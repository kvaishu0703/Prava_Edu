1. Download and fill a template

Student Excel template Staff Excel template Student CSV Staff CSV

Enter names and email addresses in the Roster sheet. Use one row per person. Leave username and enrollment_number / employee_id blank to generate unique IDs, or enter the college's existing IDs.

For students, enter the programme code (BCA, BSC-FSN or BSC-TEXTILE), pattern (2024 NEP), semester and admission year. For staff, enter the department. Contact and profile fields are optional. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD; mobile numbers should be entered as text.

Up to 200 records per file, maximum 1 MB. The preview shows generated IDs before saving. Existing usernames, emails and enrollment / employee IDs are checked to prevent duplicate accounts.

3. Confirm and download login details

After confirmation, accounts become active and an individual password is generated for each account. Download the private CSV handout once within one hour and give each person their own login details. Open the downloaded file in Excel to print or distribute individual entries.

Imported students appear in their programme and semester. Staff appear in Faculty Management; allocate subjects or class-teacher responsibility there. Teachers enter attendance and marks through their assigned classes.
