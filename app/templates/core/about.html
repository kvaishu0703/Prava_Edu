OUR COLLEGE

A foundation for
confident futures.

Established in 1997, the college is part of {{ college.society }}. It provides opportunities for women to pursue higher education and develop practical, professional and entrepreneurial skills.

The college was affiliated to SNDT Women's University from 1997 to 2016 and has been affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University since 2016, as described in its Principal's Desk.

Its undergraduate programmes include BCA (Science), Food Science & Nutrition, and Textile Science and Designing. The college homepage also lists postgraduate study in Health Science (Dietetics) and skill development programmes.
